## Public-repository guardrails

- Official spelling is **VYTAL**.
- Never commit secrets, credentials, PHI, PII, PFS/POF, bank statements,
  restricted contracts, clinical records or confidential formulas.
- Separate facts, assumptions, proposals and recommendations.
- Use Concept, R&D, Pre-development, Draft, Review, Approved, Issued,
  Superseded or Archive as explicit stage labels.
- Do not publish medical, product, regulatory, legal, financing or opening
  claims without the designated approval and source.
