# Scope

## Mission

Public VYTAL House marketing website and stage-safe partner/member information.

## In scope

- Public-safe code, schemas, documentation, tests and approved assets for this domain.
- Cross-repository contracts referenced to `VYTALEnterprise`.
- Decision records with source, owner, date, stage and successor.

## Out of scope

- Enterprise canon that belongs in `VYTALEnterprise`.
- Live personal, clinical, financial, applicant, member or customer records.
- Claims or approvals not supported by the controlling source.

## Required media

- approved exterior/interior references
- approved floor-plan explainer
- service iconography
- public-safe team imagery

Required media are requests, not approved assets. Promotion requires source,
owner, license, project, status, approval and supersession metadata.
